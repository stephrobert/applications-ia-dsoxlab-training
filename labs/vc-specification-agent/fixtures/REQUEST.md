# Request: approvals in the purchasing portal

From: Claire, head of purchasing
To: the portail-achats team

Hello,

From the first of next month, no order should reach a supplier before the
right people have said yes. Here is what we agreed with finance.

Small orders, up to 1,000 euros (1,000 included), the buyer places alone, as
today. Above that, the buyer's manager approves the order first. The big
ones, above 10,000 euros, also go to the finance department, once the
manager has approved.

All our suppliers invoice in euros, and our thresholds are in euros. An
order always costs something. When the portal cannot tell who must approve
an order, it refuses the order: it never lets an order through by default.

Please do not touch the order module (`orders.py`) this time: the team that
owns it will call your function later, once it is tested.

Thanks,
Claire
